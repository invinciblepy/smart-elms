Support

Academic skills

Book a short session with the academic skills team if an assignment or quiz feels overwhelming. Early support is the point of Smart ELMS — a risk flag is a prompt, not a judgement.

Talk to your instructor

Use the assignment comment box to ask a clarifying question, or message your instructor after class. They can see your progress and engagement so the conversation starts from data, not guesswork.

Wellbeing

If low engagement is about wellbeing rather than the module, university counselling and student services are the right next step. The platform never stores real clinical data.

How the AI banner works

The reminder on your dashboard is produced by an interpretable model (Decision Tree or Logistic Regression) trained on synthetic data. It looks at logins, submission rate, scores and completion — not personal attributes.
